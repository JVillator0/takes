## Product review · `2-product-design/01-SPEC.md` · verdict: **major changes**

**Strengths**
- Principles are the right ones for the mission: price before action, explain every state in words, never lose a version, AI proposes and the human disposes.
- Copy rules for money (ranges, 'up to', 'Not charged', past tense after settlement) are specific enough to implement and test.
- Names three audiences (customer, operator, evaluator) and ties each to a concrete need.

**Findings**

## Product review · `2-product-design/02-SPEC.md` · verdict: **major changes**

**Strengths**
- Every failure path ends in a plain sentence and states whether money moved (P05).
- Estimate on the button, hold-based blocking, idempotent submit and keyboard shortcuts are exactly the 'clear UX around costs and errors' the brief grades.
- Drawer is URL-addressable and the AI flow is diff-then-confirm with the price visible before running (P07).

**Findings**

| # | Severity | Section | Issue | Suggestion | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | critical | P04/P08 vs 3-03 §5 (balance model) | The sheets define Available = balance minus active holds (P08 note 1: balance $10.9257 minus hold $0.1139 = $10.8118; A05 shows 'Balance' and 'On hold'). 3-03 says customers.balance_nanos = Σ ledger = already the available figure, and holds are ledger entries (hold/hold_release). **Questions for Javier**
- Which balance model should the UI and ledger share? Options: A. Wireframe model: balance = Σ money entries (purchase, promo_grant, charge, adjustment); holds are only state (held = Σ open usage max_charge); Available = balance - held. Hold/release are not ledger rows. / B. Current 3-03 model: hold and hold_release are ledger rows; balance_nanos = Available; show only Available and On hold, no gross balance. / C. Keep both: ledger has hold rows but the UI derives gross balance by excluding them. Recommendation: A. It makes the ledger a pure money record, the statement and activity foot trivially, the invariant is 'Σ ledger = balance' and held is derivable from open usage records, which is easier to explain live. Have the technical reviewer confirm the lock/race tests still hold.


## Product review · `2-product-design/03-SPEC.md` · verdict: **major changes**

**Strengths**
- The console is read-mostly with the only money write (Adjust credits) audited and gated, a safe design for an evaluator-facing demo.
- Reconciliation against GET /v1/usage per UTC day, with the billing-day rule and 9 decimals, directly proves the brief's 'costs match /v1/usage' requirement.

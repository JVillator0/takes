## Technical review · `7-admin-console/SPEC.md` · verdict: **major changes**

**Strengths**
- Scope follows D-017: resource list/view layouts are standard and the wireframes (A02-A04, A06, A09-A11) cover the custom pages, widgets and table contents. The table and widget wireframes are detailed (columns, filters, row actions, header stats), and A06 money rows are internally consistent (59,230,000 x 1.25 = 74,037,500, and A03 totals add up).
- The 20% margin as a built-in invariant check on Reports is a smart, explainable correctness signal. Aggregates are SQL sums on bigint with withCount/withSum and a no-N+1 test.
- No polling on widgets and UTC labelling are consistent with the billing-day rule and cost model. All write actions have a Shield permission, a reason and an activity-log entry.

**Findings**

| # | Severity | Section | Issue | Suggestion | Resolution |

| 1 | critical | Providers > Pixelforge usage (drill-down) / A04 | The drill-down 'takes that completed that day without a settled usage' cannot be queried locally.
